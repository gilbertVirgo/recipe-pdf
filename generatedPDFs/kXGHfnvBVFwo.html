Chocolate Cake

Serves 8

20 mins prep

45 mins cook

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. 1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. 2. Mix dry ingredients together.
  3. 3. Add wet ingredients and mix until smooth.
  4. 4. Pour into a greased baking pan.
  5. 5. Bake for 45 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.

Notes

Let the cake cool before serving. Add frosting if desired.
